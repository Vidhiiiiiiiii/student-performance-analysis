--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This analysis proves that Math and Portuguese require DIFFERENT study strategies.

   PORTUGUESE succeeds with:

4. WHY SAME STRATEGY FAILS:
   Math student -> Portuguese:
   X High study hours don't guarantee Portuguese success (weak correlation)
   X Avoiding social activities misses conversation practice
   X Grade consistency less important than motivation

   Portuguese student -> Math:
   X Irregular study patterns fail in Math (need consistency)
   X Variable grades predict failure (-0.656 correlation)
   X Motivation alone insufficient (only +0.182 correlation)

RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Schools MUST teach different study strategies for Math vs Portuguese:
- Math: Consistency, structure, regularity
- Portuguese: Motivation, engagement, conversation, recent performance
